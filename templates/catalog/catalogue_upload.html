Preparing your file

  1. Download the template and fill in its Catalogue sheet. Keep the headings in row 1.
  2. Every row needs sku and name. New parts also need sell_price. Use one row per SKU.
  3. Keep SKU, barcode and OEM numbers as Text. Enter prices and quantities as values with at most two decimal places. Formulas are not accepted.
  4. Blank optional cells preserve current values when updating. For new parts, blank costs and quantities default to zero.
  5. Opening quantity is applied once, only when the destination branch has zero stock and no movement history. To change established stock, use Stock Adjustment or purchase receipts.

See the template's Instructions sheet for optional supplier and vehicle fitment fields. Previews expire after one hour.
